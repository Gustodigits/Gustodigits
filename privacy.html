Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how GustoDigits may collect, use, disclose and protect information when you access our website and virtual-number services.

Please note: This is a general policy template. Before publishing, confirm that it accurately describes your website, database, payment providers, analytics, cookies and virtual-number suppliers. Remove any section that does not apply.

1. Introduction

GustoDigits respects privacy and aims to handle personal information responsibly. This policy describes the types of information that may be processed when you visit the website, create an account, request a virtual number or contact support.

Your information will be handled in accordance with applicable data-protection and privacy laws. This policy should be read alongside our Terms & Conditions.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, information may include:

  • Account information: such as your username, email address and account identifier.
  • Order information: such as selected country, service, order status, virtual-number order details and transaction references.
  • Payment information: such as payment status, amount, transaction reference and wallet activity. Payment card or banking details may be handled by a payment provider rather than stored directly by GustoDigits.
  • Support communications: information you choose to include when contacting us by email, WhatsApp or Telegram.
  • Technical information: where available, this may include IP address, browser type, device information, diagnostic logs and website activity.

The information actually collected depends on the website's configuration and the services you use. We should not collect information that is unnecessary for legitimate service purposes.

3. How We May Use Information

Information may be used, where appropriate, to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Process orders and provide requested virtual-number services.
  • Maintain wallet records and verify transaction status.
  • Respond to support requests and investigate reported issues.
  • Protect accounts, detect fraud and maintain platform security.
  • Diagnose technical problems and improve website functionality.
  • Meet applicable legal, regulatory and record-keeping obligations.
  • Communicate important service or policy changes.

We should use personal information only for appropriate purposes and where there is a valid legal basis to do so.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website may use cookies or similar technologies if they are configured. These technologies can help remember preferences, support account sessions, improve security or understand website performance.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect features that depend on them.

This section should be updated to identify any analytics, advertising or tracking technologies actually used by GustoDigits. Do not assume that a particular cookie or tracking tool is active unless it has been implemented.

5. When Information May Be Shared

GustoDigits may share limited information when reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including with:

  • Service providers: providers that help operate the website, database, hosting, security or customer support.
  • Number suppliers: providers involved in fulfilling a virtual-number order, where relevant.
  • Payment providers: services used to process or verify transactions.
  • Professional advisers: where necessary for legitimate business, legal or compliance purposes.
  • Authorities: where disclosure is required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

We should not sell personal information to third parties. Information should be shared only when appropriate, with suitable safeguards and in accordance with applicable law.

6. Third-Party Services

GustoDigits may rely on third-party services to provide hosting, databases, payments, virtual numbers, messaging or customer support. Those providers may process information according to their own privacy terms and practices.

When you follow a link to an external website or contact us through WhatsApp or Telegram, your use of that service is also governed by the relevant provider's terms and privacy information.

Review the privacy policies of relevant third-party providers where appropriate. GustoDigits cannot control every practice carried out independently by an external provider.

7. Data Security

We aim to use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or loss.

No website, database, transmission method or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Users should protect their passwords, use secure devices and contact support if they suspect unauthorised access to their accounts.

8. Data Retention

Personal information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including providing services, resolving disputes, maintaining appropriate transaction records and meeting legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information, its purpose and applicable law. When information is no longer required, it should be deleted or anonymised where appropriate and technically feasible.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on the laws that apply to you, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request access to personal information held about you.
  • Ask for inaccurate information to be corrected.
  • Request deletion of information where legally applicable.
  • Object to or request limits on certain processing activities.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Raise a complaint with the appropriate data-protection authority.

These rights may be subject to legal exceptions. To make a privacy request, contact us using the details at the end of this page. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

10. Children's Privacy

GustoDigits is not intended for people who are not legally permitted to use the service. We do not knowingly seek to collect children's personal information unlawfully.

If you believe a child has provided personal information in circumstances where collection is not permitted, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

11. International Data Transfers

Some service providers may process or store information in countries other than the country where you live. Where this occurs, GustoDigits should use any safeguards required by applicable data-protection law.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the platform, service providers, information-handling practices or applicable law.

Updated wording will be published on this page. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent before implementing a change.

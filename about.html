About GustoDigits

A convenient platform for accessing virtual numbers and SMS verification services, designed to make the process simple, accessible and straightforward.

Who We Are

GustoDigits is an online platform focused on providing access to virtual phone numbers for supported SMS verification and other permitted uses. Our goal is to make finding and using a suitable number easier through a straightforward online experience.

We aim to provide a clear and convenient service while encouraging responsible use, respect for privacy and compliance with applicable laws and the rules of the services our customers use.

What We Do

GustoDigits helps users access available virtual numbers for supported countries and services. Availability depends on the inventory, destination, service requested and the providers supporting each request.

  • Access to available virtual numbers for supported destinations.
  • SMS verification support for compatible services.
  • A convenient online process for requesting and managing numbers.
  • Support channels for questions and service-related issues.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make virtual-number services easier to access through a user-friendly platform, clear service information and responsive customer support.

We continuously aim to improve the customer experience while recognising that number availability, message delivery and third-party platform policies can affect the outcome of a request.

Our Commitment to Users

We aim to:

  • Present service information as clearly as possible.
  • Handle customer enquiries through our published support channels.
  • Respect user information in accordance with our Privacy Policy.
  • Explain applicable service conditions and refund eligibility.
  • Encourage lawful, responsible and authorised use of virtual numbers.

Responsible Use

GustoDigits must not be used for fraud, impersonation, harassment, unauthorised access, bypassing security controls or any other unlawful activity. Users are responsible for ensuring that their use of a number complies with applicable laws and the terms of the relevant third-party service.

Not every website or application accepts virtual numbers. We cannot guarantee that a particular third-party platform will accept a number or deliver a verification message.
